I'm a second-year undergraduate in Artificial Intelligence at MBZUAI in Abu Dhabi. I work on agentic and embodied infrastructure: the systems that let AI agents, and the models behind them, run fast and reliably on real hardware, from GPU clusters down to edge devices.

With Prof. Xiaosong Ma, I built MemArena, a benchmark for on-device personal memory assistants, accepted at NeurIPS 2026.

I also build products. I'm co-founder and CTO of MemAI, where we build interactive AI tutoring, and founder of 7D24H, a desktop agent that turns tender documents and research ideas into finished bids and papers.

Research Experience

MemArena: An Ego-Centric Benchmark for On-Device Agentic Personal Memory Assistants at Scale May 2026
Jiadong Zhang, Xiaosong Ma | Accepted at NeurIPS 2026 Datasets & Benchmarks Track; arXiv 2608.02613 MBZUAI
  • Designed and built MASim, a multi-agent simulator where persona-driven agents in a shared social world hold person-person and person-agent dialogs, with day-batched synchronization for temporal consistency.
  • Used MASim to generate a 10.3M-token benchmark (50 agents, 15 days), deriving ground truth for six dimensions from simulator state; evaluated four memory backends with five open-weight models on-device.
